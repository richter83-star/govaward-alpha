01 Executive Summary

The United States Federal Government represents the single largest enterprise spender in the world, awarding in excess of $700 Billion in contracts annually across defense, intelligence, healthcare, aerospace, and critical national infrastructure.

While contract awards to mega-cap prime contractors (e.g., Lockheed Martin, Boeing, RTX) are routinely absorbed by institutional algorithmic pricing models with negligible price impact, multi-million-dollar awards to small-cap and mid-cap publicly traded companies ($50M – $5B market capitalization) represent one of the few persistent, legally defensible, and publicly accessible information asymmetries in modern equity markets.

The Central Alpha Thesis

A $95 Million award to a defense giant with a $110B market cap is immaterial noise (0.08% of equity). The exact same $95 Million award to a small-cap drone, cybersecurity, or radar manufacturer represents 60% to 150% of annual revenue. When public markets discover the award, transformational price rerating occurs. GovAward Alpha captures this asymmetry in real time before wire syndication.

02 The Procurement Information Asymmetry

Modern quantitative trading desks spend tens of millions of dollars parsing SEC Form 8-K filings and business newswires. However, a structural lag exists between the federal award moment and broad corporate syndication:

1. Regulatory Disclosure Lag

SEC Regulation S-K Item 1.01 permits companies up to 4 business days to file Form 8-K. Furthermore, if a contractor does not deem the award technically "material" under internal legal standards, no filing is required at all.

2. Entity Name Disguises

Federal awards rarely list the parent ticker name. Contracts are assigned to joint ventures, obscure LLC subsidiaries, or local operating units (e.g., "AeroVironment California Operations LLC" rather than AVAV).

3. Unstructured Pentagon Jargon

Pentagon digests (published daily between 5:00 PM and 5:30 PM Eastern) are written in dense military prose detailing contract modifications, undefinitized actions, and delivery orders that standard news scrapers cannot reliably link to tickers.

04 5-Tier Entity Resolution & Provenance Graph

Associating raw awardee strings with public equities is achieved through a deterministic, confidence-rated 5-tier resolution cascade:

  1. Canonical String Normalization: Strips corporate entity suffixes (LLC, INC, CORP, GOVERNMENT SERVICES), removes punctuation, and standardizes joint venture formatting.
  2. Tier 1: Manual Override Matrix (MANUAL_OVERRIDE): High-priority version-controlled mapping table (manual_ticker_overrides.yaml) maintaining verified links between defense subsidiaries and parent listings.
  3. Tier 2: SEC EDGAR CIK Database (HIGH_CONFIDENCE): Exact legal entity match against the SEC's master directory of reporting issuers or high-similarity token match (Score ≥ 98).
  4. Tier 3: Guarded SEC EDGAR Fuzzy Match (MEDIUM_CONFIDENCE): Score 92–97 with head-token safeguards. Eligible for alerts; trading restricted to whitelist.
  5. Tier 4: Heuristic Search Fallback (LOW_CONFIDENCE): Third-party search guesses (yfinance symbol search). Alerting suppressed and capital execution strictly blocked.
  6. Tier 5: Unresolved Ledger (UNRESOLVED): Stored silently for operator audit via govaward unresolved.
